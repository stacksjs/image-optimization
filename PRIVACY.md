# Privacy policy

ImageOptimization processes images entirely on your Mac. It has no accounts,
analytics, advertising, tracking, or remote image processing. It does not collect
or transmit your images, filenames, image metadata, or other personal data.

The app reads images you select and writes smaller copies to your chosen output
folder. Originals remain unchanged. Image metadata, such as camera location,
can remain in the optimized copies; review it before sharing images publicly.

The native window communicates with its local processing helper through an
authenticated loopback connection on your Mac. This connection is not an upload
to an internet service. The helper stops when the app closes.

The Mac App Store version uses Apple's sandbox and native file and folder
pickers. Apple may separately collect App Store purchase, installation, and
optional crash information under Apple's privacy policy.
